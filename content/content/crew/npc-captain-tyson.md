# Captain George Tyson

> [!quote]
> "I got every one of those men home with me... This time, with this crew—we'll be safe in port by week's end."

### Background
A hardened survivor. He spent the winter of 1873 shipwrecked on an ice floe north of the 80th parallel. He survived six months of darkness eating raw seal and ensured every one of his men made it home alive.

He is confident that the *Electra* voyage will be safe compared to that experience.

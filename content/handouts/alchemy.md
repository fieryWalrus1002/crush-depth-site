# Strange Alchemical Notes

> [!warning] The written word is no longer reliable
> The names on maps are incorrect, equipment manuals have passages from a diary interpolated into them, and every dial on the engine reads ANNA.
> This is the collected writings, which seem to be the diary of an alchemist obsessed with the sea.

### Segment 2: The River
```
I thought we might direct the river into
every home, and let it run there in each,
a fountain fresh and sweet. And when
they said no I thought to go down under
it myself. And now here I am, an old
innkeeper who watches it run by
unchanged every day. If only they had
let me! When I was hip deep in the river
with twenty more feet of it overhead!
And talking to princes!
```

### Segment 5: The Living Alembic
```
A living instrument, the machinery of
the tides. Solutions, filtrations,
putrefactions, reverberations. Purpose,
intention, endeavor.
```

### Segment 6: The Great Work
```
To travel in this country: the greatest of
all and most secret.

I have sent to Prague for my ancient books.
There is no eternity in fire and heat!

This is the mistake that has strangled
our art. The greatest alchemists have
been misled! All is water, all is motion. I
shall take the sea as my alembic and the
souls within to do the great work.
Solution, exaltation, sublimation
I was wrong I was wrong I was wrong
```

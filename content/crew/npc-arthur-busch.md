# Arthur Busch

> [!quote]
> "As you can see, we've perfected the electric engine—I dare say we've surpassed Holland's in efficiency and power."

### Background
The designer of the *Electra*. He is ambitious and proud, believing his designs have surpassed John Philip Holland's work. He is eager to sell these boats to the British Admiralty and often gets impatient with the crew's "cautious" handling of his machine.
